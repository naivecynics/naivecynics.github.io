I come from Foshan, Guangdong Province, where people are obsessed with the unadulterated umami of fresh ingredients.

I am completing my B.E. in Computer Science at the College of Engineering, **SUSTech**, and will join **Georgia Tech** as an incoming M.S. student in Music Technology in August 2026.

I am deeply passionate about this discipline, especially the emerging field of deep learning. I enjoy the process of transforming abstract ideas into rigorous and elegant paradigms.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **music information retrieval**, **language models**, and **symbolic music**. I am particularly interested in music-language learning, symbolic music modeling, and cross-modal generation and transcription.

I enjoy building systems that connect musical structure, performance, notation, and language in a unified learning framework.
